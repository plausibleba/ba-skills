# Step 01: ValueStream Discovery — Risk Assessment & Response

## ValueStream Identity

**Name:** Risk Assessment & Response
**Domain:** Prudential Supervision of Financial Institutions
**Scope:** The central hub of all supervisory activities. Maintains and updates the risk model for each regulated entity, consolidates findings from all supervisory activities, determines risk ratings, and selects the appropriate supervisory response stance.

## Value Proposition

**Title:** Evidence-Based Supervisory Positioning
**Statement:** Enables the regulator to maintain a current, defensible assessment of each regulated entity's risk profile and to calibrate supervisory intensity proportionate to that risk — ensuring that supervisory resources are deployed where they will have the greatest protective effect for depositors, policyholders, and financial system stability.

## Trigger & Terminal Outcome

**Trigger:** New findings from any supervisory activity (examination, off-site analysis, engagement) OR scheduled periodic risk model review OR material change in entity circumstances or macroeconomic conditions.

**Terminal Outcome:** Updated risk ratings and supervisory response stance confirmed. Supervisory Action Plan approved and activated. Downstream value streams notified of any changes to supervisory intensity.

## Stakeholders

- **Supervisory Analyst** — primary operator of the risk model and SAP
- **Senior Supervisor / Team Lead** — review authority, risk judgement calibration
- **Chief Supervisor / Division Head** — approval authority for significant rating changes
- **Executive Leadership** — escalation point for systemically important entities
- **Specialist Risk Teams** — provide domain expertise (credit, market, operational, insurance risk)
- **Legal & Compliance** — advise on enforcement triggers and delegation authorities
- **Regulated Entity (Board & Senior Management)** — recipient of supervisory communications
- **Peer Regulators** — cross-jurisdictional coordination for multinational entities

## Scope Boundaries

**In scope:**
- Intake and triage of findings from all supervisory value streams
- Entity risk model maintenance and update
- Inherent risk assessment across risk categories
- Management and control quality evaluation
- Risk rating calculation and determination
- Supervisory response stance calibration
- Supervisory Action Plan formulation
- Review and approval of ratings and SAP
- Communication of approved positions
- Monitoring triggers for reassessment

**Out of scope:**
- Conducting examinations (VS-03)
- Off-site data collection and analysis (VS-02)
- Formal enforcement actions (VS-06)
- Regulatory approvals processing (VS-07)
- Supervisory planning and resource allocation (VS-01)
- Prudential standard-setting and rule-making

## Source Evidence

This value stream definition is grounded in structural patterns observed across:
- APRA Supervisory Review Project (SRP) documentation — six core supervisory processes with risk assessment and response as the hub
- APRA probability and impact rating methodology — inherent risk, management & control, net risk, capital support categories
- APRA graduated supervisory response system — four-stance escalation framework
- NY Fed Supervision Group — risk-focused supervisory approach, LISCC governance for systemically important firms
- NY Fed value stream stages — Institutional Examination, Risk Assessment, Enforcement Actions lifecycle
- OSFI Business Architecture Framework — supervisory risk tiering and staged intervention
- Federal Reserve capability hierarchy — Institutional Oversight, Risk Management, Enforcement Actions capability decomposition
