# Step 02: Lifecycle Stages — Risk Assessment & Response

## Stage Sequence

### Stage 1: Intake & Triage
**Purpose:** Receive, categorise, and prioritise incoming findings, signals, and triggers that require risk model attention.
**Entry Signal:** New finding arrives from any supervisory value stream (examination report, quarterly risk review, engagement observation, enforcement status update, market event, entity-initiated disclosure).
**Exit Signal:** Finding categorised, prioritised, and assigned to appropriate risk analyst for processing.
**Key Activities:** Log finding source and type, categorise by risk domain, assess urgency, assign to entity risk analyst, link to existing risks/issues if applicable.
**Evidence Basis:** APRA SRP describes risks/issues at three levels (industry, entity key, entity non-key) with triggers from both baseline and reactive activities. NY Fed uses risk-focused intake customised to firm risk profile.

### Stage 2: Risk Model Update
**Purpose:** Reassess the entity's risk profile by updating inherent risk scores, management and control quality assessments, and significance weightings across all relevant risk categories.
**Entry Signal:** Prioritised finding assigned for risk model processing.
**Exit Signal:** Risk model updated with current assessments across all affected categories.
**Key Activities:** Review inherent risk across applicable categories (governance, management, risk governance, strategy, liquidity, operational risk, credit risk, market risk, insurance risk where applicable). Evaluate management and control quality. Adjust significance weightings. Calculate net risk positions. Assess capital support adequacy.
**Evidence Basis:** APRA probability and impact rating methodology defines 12 risk/capital categories with inherent risk, management & control quality, net risk, and capital support dimensions. All regulators maintain multi-category risk models with weighted scoring.

### Stage 3: Rating Determination
**Purpose:** Calculate composite risk ratings from the updated risk model — probability of failure, impact of failure, and overall risk of failure.
**Entry Signal:** Risk model update completed across all affected categories.
**Exit Signal:** Proposed risk ratings calculated and documented with supporting rationale.
**Key Activities:** Calculate probability of failure from net risk scores. Determine impact of failure from entity systemic importance. Derive overall risk of failure composite. Compare to previous ratings to identify material changes. Document rating rationale and supporting evidence.
**Evidence Basis:** All four regulators maintain composite rating frameworks that combine probability and impact dimensions. Rating changes above materiality thresholds trigger escalated review and approval requirements.

### Stage 4: Response Calibration
**Purpose:** Map the entity's risk rating to the appropriate supervisory response stance and determine the required supervisory intensity.
**Entry Signal:** Proposed risk ratings available.
**Exit Signal:** Supervisory response stance determined with prescribed activity levels.
**Key Activities:** Apply response framework to map ratings to supervisory stance (e.g., normal monitoring, heightened oversight, mandated corrective action, restructure preparation). Determine baseline supervisory activities appropriate to the stance. Identify any stance changes from previous cycle. Flag entities crossing stance boundaries for enhanced governance.
**Evidence Basis:** APRA graduated response system defines four stances with prescribed activity types. OSFI uses staged intervention framework. NY Fed tailors supervisory programmes to firm complexity and risk profile. All regulators implement graduated, proportionate response.

### Stage 5: Action Plan Formulation
**Purpose:** Produce the Supervisory Action Plan (SAP) that specifies the risks, issues, activities, and obligations for the entity in the current supervisory cycle.
**Entry Signal:** Supervisory response stance confirmed.
**Exit Signal:** Draft SAP completed with risks, activities, timelines, and entity obligations specified.
**Key Activities:** Document key risks/issues with current status. Specify baseline activities for the entity's stance. Add reactive activities triggered by specific findings. Define entity-specific actions with obligation levels (advisory, expected, required, directed). Set timelines and milestones. Link activities to responsible supervisory teams and downstream value streams.
**Evidence Basis:** APRA SAP is a management report showing risk ratings, key risks/issues, and key activities at a point in time. All regulators produce equivalent supervisory programmes or letters specifying expected activities and entity obligations.

### Stage 6: Review & Approval
**Purpose:** Subject the risk model update, proposed ratings, and SAP to formal quality assurance and authority approval, governed by delegation frameworks.
**Entry Signal:** Draft SAP and supporting materials ready for review.
**Exit Signal:** Ratings and SAP formally approved by delegated authority.
**Key Activities:** Submit bundled review package (risk model, ratings, SAP). Route to eligible reviewers based on delegation rules (determined by entity risk rating, rating history, entity location, nature of changes). Conduct peer review or quality check. Escalate to higher authority if required (significant rating changes, systemically important entities, stance boundary crossings). Handle absence/timeout escalation procedures.
**Evidence Basis:** APRA review and approval process explicitly governed by delegation authorities from Treasury with structured escalation. NY Fed three-lines-of-defence model and LISCC committee governance. All regulators require formal sign-off on risk assessments with authority levels proportionate to significance.

### Stage 7: Communication & Activation
**Purpose:** Communicate approved ratings and supervisory positions to internal teams and the regulated entity, and activate the SAP as the working supervisory programme.
**Entry Signal:** Ratings and SAP approved.
**Exit Signal:** All stakeholders notified, SAP activated, downstream value streams triggered as required.
**Key Activities:** Notify supervisory teams of any changes to entity stance or activity requirements. Trigger activity scheduling in downstream value streams (examinations, engagements, enforcement). Communicate to regulated entity where required (supervisory letter, formal notification of stance change). Update supervisory systems and records. Publish to internal management reporting.
**Evidence Basis:** APRA prudential review and consultation processes include formal letters to entities. NY Fed supervisory teams receive programme updates through LISCC and portfolio governance. All regulators maintain formal communication channels for supervisory positions.

### Stage 8: Monitoring & Reassessment Trigger
**Purpose:** Ongoing monitoring of the entity against the active SAP, tracking remediation progress and watching for new risk signals that would trigger re-entry to the cycle.
**Entry Signal:** SAP activated and monitoring begins.
**Exit Signal:** Reassessment triggered (scheduled review date reached, material new finding received, or macroeconomic event) — cycle re-enters at Stage 1.
**Key Activities:** Track entity progress against SAP milestones and obligations. Monitor exception reports and early warning indicators. Receive interim findings from ongoing supervisory activities. Assess whether new information is material enough to trigger early reassessment. At scheduled review date, initiate next cycle.
**Evidence Basis:** APRA SRP describes continuous feedback loop where all processes return to risk assessment and response. The risk model must be "constantly revisited and revised with each change in the entity's circumstance and with each macroeconomic change." All regulators maintain ongoing monitoring between formal assessment cycles.
